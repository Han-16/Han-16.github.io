Hi! I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Chicago, where I am fortunate to be advised by Alexander R. Block. Before joining UIC, I earned my B.B.A. and M.S. from Kookmin University, where I had the privilege of studying under Professors Jihye Kim and Hyunok Oh.

Research

I am interested in using cryptography to address real-world security and privacy challenges. I am particularly drawn to zk-SNARK, which allow us to verify claims without revealing sensitive information. My research interests include designing more efficient proof systems and developing privacy-preserving AI technologies.
